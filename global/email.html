Lesson

A definition

Customer Success is the proactive management of the customer relationship, so that customers reach the result they bought the product for — and keep reaching it. Support reacts to problems; CS works ahead of them.

Where it came from

When software was sold once, the deal ended at the sale. Subscriptions changed the economics: revenue now depends on whether the customer stays and grows. Companies needed a function that owns that outcome — and Customer Success appeared.
