Lesson

A definition

Customer Success is the systematic way a company grows revenue through the value customers get from the product.

In simple words: the link between the customer and the product. A guide — to the result for the customer, and to revenue for the company.

How it evolved

It started with cloud and subscriptions. A customer can now leave any month — so keeping them became the whole company's job.

CS 1.0
Reactive · early 2000s
Answering complaints when they come
CS 2.0
Proactive · late 2000s
Retention, renewals, working on churn
CS 3.0
Strategic · 2010s
A strategic role in the company
CS 4.0
Digital · early 2020s
Digital tools and analytics
CS 5.0
AI-first · now
AI in every process — and the right balance of AI and people

What CS is accountable for

01Lower churn
02Deeper product usage
03More renewals
04Cross-sell & upsell
05Revenue per customer

Put together, that's the key idea of this lesson: Customer Success is a revenue centre, not a cost.
