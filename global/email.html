1LEARN · THE DEFINITION
Customer Success is how a company grows revenue through the value customers get from its product.
In simple words: the link between the customer and the product.
2LEARN · WHERE IT CAME FROM

CS was born out of churn

1996CRM vendor Vantive is losing customers. It hires Marie Alexander — a former client — who builds the first department called Customer Success: a team that asks each customer how they define success.
2005Salesforce is growing fast — and losing about 8% of customers a month. Keeping customers becomes as important as winning new ones, and CS becomes part of the company's DNA.
WhyCloud and subscriptions changed the rules: a customer can leave in any month. Revenue now depends on whether customers stay and grow.

Five eras of CS — what changed in each

IMAGE BLOCKED · ALT TEXT SHOWN
Staircase chart of five eras: CS 1.0 Reactive (early 2000s), 2.0 Proactive, 3.0 Strategic, 4.0 Digital, 5.0 AI-first (now).
CS 1.0Reactive. Answer complaints when customers write in. CS 2.0Proactive. Reach out first: renewals, retention, churn. CS 3.0Strategic. CS gets a seat at the leadership table. CS 4.0Digital. Health scores, CS platforms, data-driven work. CS 5.0AI-first. AI in every process — people for judgment and trust.
3LEARN · WHAT IT OWNS

Five things CS is accountable for

IMAGE BLOCKED · ALT TEXT SHOWN
Five goals of Customer Success: lower churn, deeper product usage, more renewals, cross-sell and upsell, higher revenue per customer.

Lower churn · deeper product usage · more renewals · cross-sell and upsell · higher revenue per customer.

So Customer Success is a revenue centre — not a cost.
